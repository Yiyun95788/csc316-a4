Understanding the Features

Energy

Intensity and activity level. High energy tracks feel fast, loud, and noisy.

Valence

Musical positivity. High valence sounds happy and cheerful, low valence sounds sad or angry.

Danceability

How suitable a track is for dancing based on tempo, rhythm stability, and beat strength.

Acousticness

Confidence measure of whether the track is acoustic. Higher values indicate more acoustic instruments.

Speechiness

Presence of spoken words. Values above 0.66 are likely spoken word content like podcasts.

Tempo

Overall pace of the track in beats per minute (BPM). Average tempo is around 120 BPM.

Key Insights from the Dataset

The Happy-Energetic Correlation

Songs with high energy tend to have higher valence. Energetic music is often uplifting and positive.

Danceability Sweet Spot

Most danceable tracks cluster around moderate energy levels (0.5-0.8) rather than extreme highs.

Acoustic vs. Electric

Acoustic tracks typically have lower energy and danceability compared to electronic productions.

Popularity Patterns

Popular songs span all mood quadrants, but tend to cluster in the moderate-to-high energy range.
